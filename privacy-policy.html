Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

This policy explains how Amiral Consultant Engineers handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and project details you provide. Our hosting and security providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate requested services, communicate about potential or active projects, maintain website security, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

How information is disclosed

Information may be provided to service providers that support website hosting, storage, security, and communications, subject to their applicable obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legal rights and safety. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including business, legal, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

California privacy rights

Where California privacy law applies, residents may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, and to receive information about how it is used or disclosed. Exercising a privacy right will not result in unlawful discrimination.

Your choices and requests

You may request information about your submitted data, ask for a correction, or request deletion by using the website contact form. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and legal exceptions may apply.

Children and external links

This website is intended for business and project inquiries and is not directed to children under 13. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' own privacy practices.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

This policy is general website information and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel for the firm's specific operations and compliance obligations.
