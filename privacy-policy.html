Privacy Policy

Draft effective date: November 5, 2026

Amiral Consultant Engineers, Inc. ("Amiral," "we," "us") respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This notice explains what information may be processed when you visit the site or contact us.

Information you choose to share

The current website does not offer visitor accounts or an on-site submission form. If you call the phone number listed on this website, you may choose to provide your name, telephone number, project information, and other details. Calls and voicemail may be processed by our telecommunications service providers. We do not store this information in a website contact-form database.

Technical information and third-party services

Our website is designed to be served through a hosting provider. The host may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps, and security logs under its own practices. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts; requesting those resources involves a connection to Google's servers. We do not control the data processed by external services.

This version of the site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run analytics scripts. Browser or hosting-provider behavior may differ, and new services must be disclosed here before being enabled.

How information is used

Information provided in phone calls, voicemail, or subsequent business communications may be used to answer questions, evaluate requested engineering services, coordinate with you, maintain business correspondence, and satisfy applicable professional, legal, or recordkeeping obligations.

Sharing and retention

We may need to share information from an inquiry with service providers or project collaborators when reasonably necessary to address your request, subject to applicable confidentiality commitments. We may also disclose information if legally required. Retention depends on the inquiry, legal obligations, and project recordkeeping needs. No fixed retention period is promised in this website draft.

Your choices and requests

You may call us at 858.610.4469 to ask about information you have supplied or request a correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal exceptions. If California privacy laws grant you additional rights, those rights will be honored as applicable. Do not provide sensitive personal identification or financial records through ordinary calls or voicemail unless specifically needed and secure delivery arrangements have been made.

California privacy rights

Some California privacy rights, including rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, apply to businesses meeting statutory requirements. This statement does not assert that Amiral meets or does not meet those thresholds. If those requirements apply, the site notice and request procedures must be supplemented with the additional required disclosures and mechanisms.

Do Not Track and external links

No website feature has been implemented specifically to respond to browser Do Not Track signals. Links to third-party sites, including the international Amiral site, are governed by the privacy practices of those sites.

Policy updates

We may revise this statement as site features or practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
