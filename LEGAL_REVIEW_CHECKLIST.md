# Legal publication checklist (drafts, not legal advice)

Before publishing, confirm with the business owner and counsel:

1. Full legal business name, responsible entity, website domain, jurisdiction, and contact details.
2. What hosting provider logs are recorded and for how long; whether any analytics, pixels, embedded videos, cookies, CRM, or online forms have been added.
3. Whether Google Fonts is loaded remotely (current website: yes) and whether it should be locally hosted instead.
4. Actual practices for telephone inquiries, voicemail, subsequent correspondence, retention, sharing with consultants, and rights request handling.
5. Whether CCPA/CPRA statutory thresholds apply and, if so, add the mandated categories, lookback disclosures, rights procedure, and necessary opt-out mechanisms.
6. Verify rights and permissions for portfolio photographs, illustrations, client names, and historical employer trademarks.
7. Confirm that the no-engineer-client-relationship language aligns with signed engagement agreements and licensing obligations.
8. Audit accessibility with a keyboard, screen reader, and mobile device; the Accessibility Statement is not a WCAG compliance certification.
9. Replace 'Draft effective date' with actual publication date after review.

Included pages: privacy-policy.html, terms-of-use.html, engineering-disclaimer.html, accessibility.html.

Reference starting points: California OAG CalOPPA guidance, California Privacy Protection Agency CCPA guidance.

10. Confirm the published phone number is monitored for privacy requests, accessibility feedback, and other legal notices; consider a dedicated business contact channel if required.
