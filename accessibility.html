Accessibility Statement

Draft effective date: November 5, 2026

Amiral Consultant Engineers aims to make this website accessible and usable for a broad range of visitors, including people using mobile devices, keyboards, or assistive technology.

Accessibility approach

The site uses semantic headings, descriptive links, visible keyboard focus indicators, and responsive layouts. We also aim to respect reduced-motion preferences where practical. These measures are ongoing goals, not a certification that every page or feature fully conforms to any particular WCAG level.

Known considerations

The portfolio contains horizontally navigated image cards, and the hero includes automatically changing photographs. Visitors who have difficulty using these features may contact us for the same project information in an alternative format.

Feedback and alternative access

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email ben.sadawi@amiralce.com and describe the page, device or assistive technology, and what you were trying to do. We will review your request and work to provide the information in an accessible way.

Updates

We may revise the site and this statement as accessibility improvements are implemented.
