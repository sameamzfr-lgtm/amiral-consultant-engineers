02 / Track record

Two decades of
global delivery.

Before founding Amiral, Ben Sadawi managed design and construction delivery for civil, institutional, and large-scale infrastructure programs across Canada, South America, Southern California, and the Emirates.

  1. 2003–2005

    SNC-Lavalin GroupSenior Project Engineer · Toronto, Ontario

    Delivered civil and environmental engineering across feasibility, design, and construction supervision for mining, nuclear, and industrial processing facilities, including water supply, stormwater, and tailings systems.

    • Downsview National Park
    • Las Cristinas Gold Mine, Venezuela
    • Pascua-Lama Gold Mine, Chile
    • Port Hope Nuclear Plant Drainage
  2. 2005–2006

    Hunsaker & AssociatesProject Manager · Los Angeles, CA

    Managed private development deliverables and jurisdictional approvals for Southern California land development, overseeing site layout, grading, utilities, and stormwater design.

    • ANAVERDE Residential Development, Palmdale
  3. 2006–2008

    PENCO Engineering, Inc.Project Manager · Irvine, CA

    Managed private residential, institutional, and commercial projects across San Diego, Orange, and Los Angeles Counties totaling an estimated $250 million in construction value.

    • Valley Region High School #4, LAUSD
    • Tustin Medical Tower (LEED Silver)
    • Glendale Community College Reconstruction
    • Verizon Wireless Building Addition
  4. 2008–2013

    AECOMSenior Project Manager & Unit Leader · USA / UAE

    Managed design and construction delivery and built AECOM's Stakeholder Management Unit for large-scale residential, institutional, and regional infrastructure programs, including multi-billion-dollar developments across the Emirates.

    • Abu Dhabi Metro
    • Etihad Union Railway
    • Abu Dhabi Airport Midfield Terminal Complex
    • Zayed University
    • Saadiyat Island Cultural District
  5. 2013–2023

    MMFX Technologies / CMCSenior Engineering & Project Leadership

    Continued senior engineering and project leadership roles, including private development work across California through 2022.

  6. Present

    Amiral Consultant EngineersManaging Partner & Principal Engineer · San Diego, CA

    Bringing two decades of global infrastructure and program management experience to residential and commercial projects across Greater San Diego.
